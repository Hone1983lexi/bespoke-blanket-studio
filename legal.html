Privacy policy

Bespoke Blanket Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information may be collected when you contact us, request a quote or place an order, why we use it and how we protect it.

Information we may collect

  • Name and contact details such as email address and telephone number.
  • Information you provide about your blanket, including colours, size, design, stitch pattern and personalisation requirements.
  • Order, quotation and correspondence details.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the website, such as basic server request information.

Why we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotations.
  • To fulfil and administer orders.
  • To communicate about an existing enquiry or order.
  • To maintain business records and meet legal or accounting obligations.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.

Lawful bases

Depending on the purpose, processing may be necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract, to perform a contract, to comply with a legal obligation, or for legitimate interests such as running and securing the business. Where consent is required, we will ask for it separately and you can withdraw it.

Sharing information

We only share personal information where reasonably necessary, for example with service providers who help operate the website, email, payment, delivery or accounting functions, or where the law requires us to do so. We do not sell customer personal information.

Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including applicable legal, tax and accounting requirements. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, and to data portability in certain circumstances. If processing relies on consent, you can withdraw consent. Contact us using the details above.

Complaints about privacy

Please contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Cookie policy

Terms & conditions

About our service

Bespoke Blanket Studio makes handmade crochet blankets to order. Designs, colours, stitch patterns, sizes and personalisation are agreed with the customer before production.

Quotes and prices

A website enquiry or quote request is not itself acceptance of an order. We will confirm the specification, price, payment arrangements and estimated production/delivery timeframe before a paid order is accepted.

Where a price cannot reasonably be calculated in advance, the basis on which it will be calculated will be explained before the customer is bound by the contract. Any optional extras must be clearly agreed.

Design and colour variations

Handmade crochet is produced individually. Yarn batches, screens and lighting can cause minor differences in colour and texture. We will use reasonable care to make the finished blanket match the agreed specification.

Payment

Payment arrangements, including any deposit or balance payment, will be confirmed in writing before production begins. No payment terms on this page remove or limit statutory consumer rights.

Legal rights

Nothing in these terms excludes or restricts rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted under UK consumer law. Goods must be as described, of satisfactory quality and fit for any particular purpose agreed as applicable.

Bespoke and personalised orders

Customers can request a blanket made around their chosen specification. We will confirm the final design, colours, dimensions, stitch pattern and personalisation before production.

Because a genuinely bespoke or personalised item may be made to the customer's specifications or clearly personalised, the normal 14-day change-of-mind cancellation right for distance sales may not apply to that item. This does not affect rights relating to faulty, damaged, misdescribed or otherwise non-conforming goods.

If production has not started and you want to change an agreed specification, contact us as soon as possible. We will confirm whether the change is practical and whether it changes the agreed price or delivery date.

Delivery

Estimated production and delivery times will be confirmed before a paid order is accepted. Handmade items can take longer than mass-produced goods, and the agreed timeframe will depend on the blanket's size, complexity, yarn availability and current workload.

Delivery charges and the delivery method will be confirmed before the order is placed. If circumstances outside our reasonable control affect production or delivery, we will communicate with you and agree an appropriate solution.

Returns, cancellations and refunds

For standard goods sold at a distance, consumers generally have a statutory 14-day cancellation period after receiving the goods, subject to the legal exceptions. Where an item is genuinely made to the customer's specifications or clearly personalised, the bespoke-goods exception may apply.

Nothing here removes statutory remedies for goods that are faulty, not as described, or otherwise fail to meet the requirements of consumer law. If there is a problem, contact us promptly with details and photographs where helpful so we can investigate and offer the appropriate remedy.

Where a refund is legally due, we will process it within the applicable legal timeframe and using the original payment method where required.

Complaints

We want problems resolved quickly and fairly. Please email hello@bespokeblanketstudio.co.uk with your name, order/quote reference if available, a description of the issue and what outcome you are seeking.

We will acknowledge the complaint, investigate it and explain the proposed resolution. We will not remove or restrict any statutory consumer rights.

Accessibility

We aim to make Bespoke Blanket Studio usable by as many people as possible. We use readable text, meaningful headings, keyboard-accessible controls and descriptive image text where practical. If you have difficulty using any part of the site, please contact us and tell us what you need.

Changes to these policies
